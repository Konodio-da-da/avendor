# Legal Review Required

The engineering implementation supports compliance controls but does not create legal compliance by itself. Human review is required before production for:

- The legal identity, registered address, registration number, support contact, and privacy contact of the operating entity.
- Whether the platform may use the terms `escrow`, `protected`, `settlement`, `payout`, `wallet`, or similar financial language for the actual Paystack arrangement.
- The exact relationship between the platform, Paystack, vendors, customers, couriers, and any other payment/logistics providers.
- Nigerian Data Protection Act 2023 obligations, privacy notices, controller/processor roles, international transfers, retention, and data-subject requests.
- FCCPA and applicable consumer rules for defective goods, wrong goods, delivery failure, cancellation, chargebacks, refunds, and dispute windows.
- CBN/payment rules applicable to marketplace settlement, subaccounts, split payments, balances, and vendor payouts.
- Copyright, trademark, image rights, user-content licenses, notice/takedown, and counterfeit-product processes.
- AI processing disclosures, automated decision review, outbound WhatsApp/marketing consent, and respectful debt communications.
- Terms of service, vendor policy, prohibited-products policy, refund policy, cookie policy, DPA, and dispute-resolution terms.

No legal certification, regulator approval, license, insurance, guarantee, office location, company registration number, or DPO identity should be published until verified and configured.
